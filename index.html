UNDERSTANDING CLEAN ENERGY

What is Sustainable Energy?

Sustainable energy comes from natural sources that can be renewed over time. It helps reduce pollution, protect natural resources, and create a cleaner future.

01

Renewable

Energy from sources like sunlight and wind that naturally replenish themselves.

02

Cleaner

Renewable energy produces fewer harmful emissions compared with traditional fossil fuels.

03

Future Ready

Clean energy can help build a more reliable and sustainable energy system for future generations.

02 • WIND POWER

Powering Tomorrow With The Wind

Wind energy uses the natural movements of air to generate electricity. Wind turbines capture the energy of moving wind and convert it into usable electrical power.

Explore Wind Data

HOW IT WORKS

From Wind to Electricity

01

Wind Blows

Moving air pushes the blades of a wind turbine and start them rotating.

02

Blade Rotate

The rotating blades turn a shaft connected to the turbine's generator.

03

Electricity Generated

The generator converts mechanical energy into electricity for everyday use.
